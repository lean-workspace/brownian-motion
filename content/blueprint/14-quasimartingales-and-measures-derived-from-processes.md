**Content of a process**

## Definition: Additive content {#def:addContent lean="MeasureTheory.AddContent"}

_Upstream marks this `\mathlibok`: realized in mathlib itself._

Let $E$ be a commutative monoid and $\mathcal{A}$ be a family of sets.
  An _additive content_ is a set function $\lambda : \mathcal{A} \to E$
  with value $0$ at the empty set
  which is finitely additive on $\mathcal{A}$ .

## Lemma: addContent_extension {#lem:addContent_extension lean="MeasureTheory.sum_addContent_eq_of_sUnion_eq" uses="def:addContent, def:semiRingOfSets, def:ringOfSets"}

_Upstream marks this `\mathlibok`: realized in mathlib itself._

Let $\lambda$ be an additive content on a semi-ring of sets $C$ .
  Then $\lambda$ admits a unique additive extension to the ring of sets generated by $C$ .

## Definition: Content associated with a process {#def:contentAssociatedWithProcess uses="def:addContent, def:predictableRectangles, lem:predictableRectangles_isSetSemiring, def:elementaryPredictableSet, lem:elementaryPredictableSets_isSetRing_of, lem:addContent_extension"}

Let $X : T \to \Omega \to E$, where $E$ is a Banach space,
 be an adapted process with $X_t \in L^1$ for every $t \in T$ .
The _content_ $\lambda_X$ associated with $X$
is an $E$-valued additive content on
elementary predictable sets,
given on predictable rectangles by

$$
\begin{align*}
  \lambda_X((s, t] \times F) &= \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{1}_F (X_t - X_s)],
  & s < t \text{ in } T,\ F \in \mathcal{F}_s,
  \\
  \lambda_X(\{0\} \times F) &= 0,
  & F \in \mathcal{F}_0
  \: .
\end{align*}
$$

## Lemma: contentAssociatedWithProcess_wellDefined {#lem:contentAssociatedWithProcess_wellDefined uses="def:contentAssociatedWithProcess"}

The content $\lambda_X$ associated with $X$ is uniquely defined.

### Proof {uses="lem:addContent_extension, lem:predictableRectangles_isSetSemiring, lem:elementaryPredictableSets_isSetRing_of"}

The uniqueness of $\lambda_X$ is guaranteed by [addContent_extension](#lem:addContent_extension),
since the predictable rectangles form a semi-ring of sets
([predictableRectangles_isSetSemiring](#lem:predictableRectangles_isSetSemiring)),
one that generates the ring of elementary predictable sets
([elementaryPredictableSets_isSetRing_of](#lem:elementaryPredictableSets_isSetRing_of)).

## Definition: Partition of a set {#def:partitionOfSet lean="IndexedPartition"}

_Upstream marks this `\mathlibok`: realized in mathlib itself._

An _partition_ of a set $A$ is a family of pairwise disjoint sets,
  which does not contain the empty set, and whose union is $A$ .
  For a family of sets $C$, a _$C$-partition_ of $A$ is a partition of $A$ consisting of sets in $C$ .

## Definition: Bounded variation of a content {#def:contentBoundedVariation uses="def:ringOfSets, def:addContent, def:partitionOfSet"}

An additive content $\lambda$ on a ring $\mathcal{A}$ of sets, with values in a normed space $E$,
has _bounded variation_ on a set $A \in \mathcal{A}$ if

$$
\begin{align*}
  \vert \lambda \vert(A)
  := \sup
  \Big\{
    \sum_{i=1}^n \| \lambda(A_i) \|
    \:\Big|\:
    (A_i)_{i=1}^n \text{ finite } \mathcal{A}\text{-partition of } A
  \Big\}
  < \infty
  \: .
\end{align*}
$$

## Lemma: contentBoundedVariation_equivOnGenerator {#lem:contentBoundedVariation_equivOnGenerator uses="def:contentBoundedVariation"}

We can equivalently take supremum over $C$-partitions of $A$ in the above
  definition, when $\mathcal{A}$ is generated by the semi-ring $C$ .

### Proof {uses="def:partitionOfSet, lem:setRing_of_finiteUnions, lem:semiRingOfSets_disjoint_of_union, def:contentBoundedVariation"}

This follows (by triangle inequality and additivity of the content)
  from the [Partition of a set](#def:partitionOfSet),
  and from the Lemmata [semiRingOfSets_disjoint_of_union](#lem:semiRingOfSets_disjoint_of_union),
  [setRing_of_finiteUnions](#lem:setRing_of_finiteUnions).

## Lemma: contentEquivElementaryIntegral {#lem:contentEquivElementaryIntegral uses="def:contentAssociatedWithProcess, def:elementaryPredictableSet, def:elemStochIntegralBilin"}

The content $\lambda_X$ associated with a process $X$ satisfies,
  for every elementary predictable set $A\subseteq (0, t] \times \Omega$,

$$
\begin{align*}
  \lambda_X(A) = \mathbb{E}[(\mathbb{1}_A \bullet X)_t]
  \: .
\end{align*}
$$

### Proof {uses="def:simpleProcess, def:elemStochIntegralBilin, lem:elementaryPredictableSet_iff_indicator"}

By [elementaryPredictableSet_iff_indicator](#lem:elementaryPredictableSet_iff_indicator),
  the process $\mathbb{1}_A$ is a simple one,

$$
\begin{align*}
  (\mathbb{1}_A)_t
  =
  \mathbb{1}_{F_0}\mathbb{1}_{\{0\}}(t) + \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{1}_{F_i} \mathbb{1}_{(s_i, t_i]}(t)
  \:,
\end{align*}
$$

  where $(s_i, t_i]$ may be taken to be pairwise disjoint,
  thanks to the Lemma [simpleProcess_admitsDisjointIntervals](#lem:simpleProcess_admitsDisjointIntervals).
Then 

$$
\begin{align*}
  \mathbb{E}[(\mathbb{1}_A \bullet X)_t]
  &= \mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{1}_{F_i} (X_{t \wedge t_i} - X_{t \wedge s_i})\right]
  \\
  &= \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{E}\left[\mathbb{1}_{F_i} (X_{t \wedge t_i} - X_{t \wedge s_i})\right]
  \\
  &= \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_X((s_i, t_i] \times F_i)
  = \lambda_X(A),
\end{align*}
$$

taking into account that for all $i$ we have
$t\wedge t_i = t_i$, $t\wedge s_i = s_i$,
as $A\subseteq (0, t] \times \Omega$ .

**Quasi-martingales**

## Definition: Quasi-martingale {#def:Quasimartingale uses="def:contentAssociatedWithProcess, def:contentBoundedVariation"}

Let $E$ be a Banach space.
An adapted integrable process $X : T \to \Omega \to E$ is called a
_quasi-martingale_ if the additive content $\lambda_X$ (on elementary predictable sets)
associated with $X$ has bounded variation on every set $(0, t] \times \Omega$ with $t \in T$ , that is,

$$
\begin{align*}
  \forall t \in T, \quad \vert \lambda_X \vert\big((0, t] \times \Omega\big) < \infty
  \: .
\end{align*}
$$

## Lemma: realContentBoundedVariation_equivalent_definition {#lem:realContentBoundedVariation_equivalent_definition uses="def:Quasimartingale"}

The content $\lambda_X$ associated with a real-valued process $X$
  has bounded variation on $(0, t] \times \Omega$ if and only if the set
  $\{\mathbb{E}[(\mathbb{1}_A \bullet X)_t] \mid A \text{ elementary predictable}\}$
  is bounded.
  In particular, an adapted integrable process $X : T \to \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$
  satisfies the conditions
  of [exists_rightContinuous_modification_of_bounded_elemStochIntegral](#thm:exists_rightContinuous_modification_of_bounded_elemStochIntegral),
  iff it is a quasi-martingale.

### Proof

The above boundedness condition,

$$
\sup
  \Big\{
    |\mathbb{E}[(\mathbb{1}_A \bullet X)_t]|
    \:\Big|\:
    A \text{ elementary predictable}
  \Big\}
  <\infty
  \quad\text{for all } t\in T,
$$

  does not change after restricting the above supremum to sets
  $A$
  contained in $(0, t] \times \Omega$,
  since by
  [elemStochIntegral_stoppedIntegrator](#lem:elemStochIntegral_stoppedIntegrator),

$$
(\mathbb{1}_A \bullet X)_t
  =
  (\mathbb{1}_A \bullet X)^t_t
  =
  ((\mathbb{1}_{[0, t]}\mathbb{1}_A) \bullet X)_t
  =
  ((\mathbb{1}_{(0, t]}\mathbb{1}_A) \bullet X)_t
  \:.
$$

  Moreover, for those $A$ the [contentEquivElementaryIntegral](#lem:contentEquivElementaryIntegral) is applicable;
  in effect, we can express the boundedness condition as

$$
\sup
  \Big\{
    |\lambda_X(A)|
    \:\Big|\:
    A \text{ elementary predictable},\:
    A\subset(0,t]\times\Omega
  \Big\}
  <\infty
  \quad\text{for all } t\in T.
$$

  Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the class of predictable rectangles,
  and let us use it in the definition of bounded variation of $\lambda_X$
  (see [contentBoundedVariation_equivOnGenerator](#lem:contentBoundedVariation_equivOnGenerator)).

  Fix $t\in T$ . We aim to show that

$$
\vert \lambda_X \vert((0,t]\times\Omega)
  = \sup\Big\{ \sum_{i=1}^n | \lambda_X(A_i)|
  \:\Big|\:
    (A_i)_{i=1}^n \text{ finite } \mathcal{A}\text{-partition of } (0,t]\times\Omega \Big\}
    <\infty
$$

  holds
  if and only if

$$
\sup
  \Big\{
    |\lambda_X(A)|
    \:\Big|\:
    A \text{ elementary predictable},\:
    A\subset(0,t]\times\Omega
  \Big\}
  <\infty.
$$

Take an elementary predictable $A\subset B:=(0,t]\times\Omega$;
choose $A_1$, \ldots, $A_m\in\mathcal{A}$ to be pairwise disjoint
and such that ([semiRingOfSets_subtract_ringElem](#cor:semiRingOfSets_subtract_ringElem))

$$
B\setminus A = \bigcup_{j=1}^m A_j.
$$

Express $A$ as a disjoint union of some $A_{m+1}$, \ldots, $A_n\in\mathcal{A}$
([elementaryPredictableSets_asDisjointUnions](#lem:elementaryPredictableSets_asDisjointUnions)).
Clearly, $(A_i)_{i=1}^n$ is a finite $\mathcal{A}$-partition of $B=(0,t]\times\Omega$, so,
by content's additivity,

$$
|\lambda_X(A)|
=
\left|
  \sum_{i=m+1}^n \lambda_X(A_i)
\right|
\le
\sum_{i=1}^n |\lambda_X(A_i)|.
$$

This shows the "only if" part.

For the "if" direction, take a sequence $((A_i^k)_{i=1}^{n_k})_{k=1}^\infty$
of finite $\mathcal{A}$-partitions of $(0,t]\times\Omega$
such that

$$
\lim_{k\to\infty}
  \sum_{i=1}^{n_k} | \lambda_X(A_i^k)|
  =\infty.
$$

Let

$$
J_k = \{i \mid 1\le i\le n_k, \: \lambda_X(A_i^k) > 0\},
$$

and note that $A_k = \bigcup_{i\in J_k} A_i^k$ is an elementary predictable
subset of $(0,t]\times\Omega$ .
Without loss of generality, we may assume that

$$
\limsup_{k\to\infty}
  \sum_{i\in J_k} \lambda_X(A_i^k)
  =
  \infty,
$$

which by additivity means

$$
\limsup_{k\to\infty}
  \lambda_X(A_k)
  =
  \infty.
$$

**Monotone processes**

## Definition: StieltjesFunction.measure {#def:StieltjesFunction.measure lean="StieltjesFunction.measure"}

_Upstream marks this `\mathlibok`: realized in mathlib itself._

Let $f : T \to \mathbb{R}$ be a right-continuous monotone function on a conditionally complete linear order $T$.
We denote by $d_+ f$ the measure on $T$ defined by $d_+ f((a, b]) = f(b) - f(a)$ for all $a, b \in T$ .

## Lemma: StieltjesFunction.measurable_measure {#lem:StieltjesFunction.measurable_measure uses="def:StieltjesFunction.measure"}

Let $X : T \to \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a right-continuous adapted process which is monotone in the time variable.
Then the measure $d_+ X_\omega$ is measurable in $\omega$ .

**Bounded variation processes**

## Definition: BoundedVariationOn.vectorMeasure {#def:BoundedVariationOn.vectorMeasure uses="def:BoundedVariationOn"}

_Upstream marks this `\mathlibok`: realized in mathlib itself._

Let $f : T \to E$ be a function of bounded variation on a suitable order $T$, with $E$ a complete normed additive group.
Then there exists a signed measure $df$ on $T$ defined by $df((a, b]) = f(b^+) - f(a^+)$ .

## Lemma: BoundedVariationOn.measurable_vectorMeasure {#lem:BoundedVariationOn.measurable_vectorMeasure uses="def:BoundedVariationOn.vectorMeasure"}

Let $X : T \to \Omega \to E$ be an adapted process which is of bounded variation in the time variable.
Then the measure $dX_\omega$ is measurable in $\omega$ .

## Lemma: eVariationOn_eq_totalVariation {#lem:eVariationOn_eq_totalVariation uses="def:BoundedVariationOn.vectorMeasure, def:eVariationOn"}

Denoting by $\vert df \vert$ the total variation of the measure $df$ ,
$V_f([a, b]) = \vert df \vert([a, b])$ .

## Lemma: BoundedVariationOn.vectorMeasure_of_monotone {#lem:BoundedVariationOn.vectorMeasure_of_monotone uses="def:BoundedVariationOn.vectorMeasure, def:StieltjesFunction.measure"}

If $f : T \to \mathbb{R}$ is a right-continuous monotone function, then the measure $df$ coincides with the measure $d_+ f$ .

**Integrable processes**

Integrable -> content on the ring of sets generated by predictable rectangles.
Can be extended to a measure on the predictable $\sigma$-algebra if the process is of class DL and ... (see Metivier, Semimartingales, 13.3).

Let $X : T \to \Omega \to E$ be a stochastic process such that $X_t$ is integrable for all $t$ .
For $s < t$ and $A \in \mathcal{F}_s$, let

$$
\begin{align*}
  \mu_X((s, t] \times A) = \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{1}_A (X_t - X_s)]
  \: .
\end{align*}
$$

Set $\mu_X(\{0\} \times A) = 0$ .
Then $\mu_X$ is a content on the predictable rectangles, which can be extended to the ring of sets it generates.
